Quality Control Checklist
Liquid Quality
  • ABV verified within ±0.2% of target
  • IBU bitterness level confirmed to recipe
  • Colour measured within approved EBC range
  • Dissolved oxygen ≤ 0.05 ppm post-fill
  • Aroma panel sign-off by brew team lead
  • Carbonation volume confirmed at fill temp
Packaging Integrity
  • Double-seam mic check across all four dimensions
  • Fill volume ±2 ml of declared quantity
  • Label placement within ±1 mm alignment
  • Best-before date inkjet legible on base
  • Batch code traceable in ERP system
  • No dents, scratches, or print defects on can
