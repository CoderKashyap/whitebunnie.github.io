PPC Services You Will Receive with White Bunnie

There are various types of pay-per-click ads available, depending on your goals:

  • Search Ads

    Paid search marketing is the most common form of PPC. Ads appear to prospects who are already searching online for your brand or industry ideal for one-time campaigns or short sales cycles.

  • Display Ads

    Display advertising reaches more than 90% of online users through Google's partner network. These visual ads appear on industry-related websites, capturing attention and encouraging action.

  • Social Ads

    One of the fastest-growing PPC segments Facebook, LinkedIn, and X all support social ads, targeting prospects by their hobbies, networks, and interests.

  • Remarketing Ads

    Remarketing can double your profits by reminding people who already visited your site to return and convert. Lower competition also makes it less expensive than search advertising.

  • Google Shopping Ads

    Perfect for stores with many products prospects see images and prices directly on the Google search results page before they even click through to your website.

  • In-Stream Ads

    Also known as YouTube ads, these run while a video is playing or alongside YouTube search results presenting your brand in a unique, memorable way.

  • Local Services Ads

    Pay-per-lead ads built for local service businesses like HVAC companies, electricians, plumbers, and locksmiths you only pay for the leads you actually receive.

  • Amazon PPC Ads

    Sponsored placements in key spots across the Amazon platform help sellers build brand awareness, increase sales, and drive more store visits.

Remember These Pointers Before Hiring the Best PPC Company

A few essentials worth checking before you commit budget to any paid search partner.

1

Choose the Right Keywords The first step is to check what your customers want. It is obvious that the best PPC services are not going to work if no one searches for your service or product. If you intend to offer a service, make sure there is a decent search volume.

2

Keep Track of Your Competitors Businesses in most industries have already optimized their campaigns through a top PPC agency. You don't have to do everything yourself since they've already done the research and determined the landing pages, ads, and keywords that work for your niche.

3

Optimize Your Copy You should always invest in the best PPC services with a strong unique selling proposition (USP). It is your USP that sets you apart from the competition and makes your business stand out.

4

Track Your Conversions You can measure the number of sales generated by your PPC campaign through conversion tracking. Use these metrics to determine which ads and keywords are actually causing customers to buy.
