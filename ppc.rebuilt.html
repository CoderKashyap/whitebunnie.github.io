What to review before hiring a PPC agency.

  1. Choose the right keywords. Validate search demand first so your ads support real audience behavior instead of wishful thinking.
  2. Keep track of competitors. Study their keywords, landing pages, and positioning to understand the bar you need to clear.
  3. Optimize your copy. A strong USP is what makes your ad stand out before the click happens.
  4. Track conversions properly. Conversion data reveals which ads and terms actually produce sales, not just traffic.
