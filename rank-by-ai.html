Answers to Your AI Ranking Questions

Everything you need to know about getting cited, ranked and recommended across AI platforms.

SEO optimizes for search engine result pages; GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) optimizes for the answers ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity generate. It shares technical SEO foundations but adds structuring content, schema and proof points so an AI model can confidently quote and recommend you.

We combine on-site work (structured data, llms.txt, clear factual content) with off-site authority building - earning mentions on the reviews, forums and publications that AI engines already pull citations from when forming an answer.

Most brands see initial movement in citations and mentions within 4-6 weeks, with stronger, more consistent AI ranking building over 3-6 months as authority signals compound - similar in pace to organic SEO, but often faster to first visibility.
